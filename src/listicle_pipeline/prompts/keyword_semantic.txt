Generate 5-8 SEMANTIC keyword variants for an SEO listicle in the category
"{category}", built around the primary keyword "{primary_keyword}".

Semantic keywords cover related topics using different words (e.g. for
project management software: "sprint planning", "resource allocation",
"team collaboration workflow"). They go in subheadings and body text to prove
topical depth, not just keyword repetition.

For each keyword, tag it with:
- relationship_type: always "semantic"
- intent_stage: "informational", "commercial_investigation", or "comparison" -
  whichever best matches how a searcher using that phrase is likely shopping
- similarity_score: 0.0-1.0, how closely it relates to the primary keyword's
  topic (not its wording)
