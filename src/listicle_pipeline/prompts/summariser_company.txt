Write listicle prose for one company section in an SEO article about
"{primary_keyword}" ({category}). Use only the facts given below - do not
invent anything beyond them.

Company facts:
- Name: {company_name}
- Positioning: {positioning}
- Starting price: {starting_price}
- Rating: {aggregated_rating}/5 ({review_count} reviews)
- What it does well: {what_it_does_well}
- Gaps: {gaps}
- Best for: {best_for}

Return:
- summary_blurb: 2-3 sentences introducing the company, its positioning, and
  what makes it distinct. Engaging but factual SEO-blog tone.
- does_well_prose: one full sentence per "what it does well" bullet, expanding
  it into natural prose (same order, same count as the input bullets).
- gaps_prose: one full sentence per gap bullet, expanding it into natural,
  even-handed prose (same order, same count as the input bullets).
- best_for_line: one sentence expanding "best for" into a natural
  recommendation sentence.

Do not mention pricing or star rating in any of these fields - those are
rendered separately.
